Reading Passage

The modern world faces unprecedented ecological challenges, primarily driven by human activities that deplete finite natural resources. As industrialization expanded, the skyrocketing level of greenhouse gas emissions initiated global climate instability. This shift has already triggered devastating weather events, such as severe droughts and unpredictable floods, leaving coastal communities extremely vulnerable to rising sea levels.

Moreover, human negligence continues to contaminate vital freshwater supplies with industrial chemicals, threatening both human populations and the fragile ecosystems that support biodiversity. In response, international groups are advocating for a comprehensive shift toward sustainability by investing heavily in renewable energy technologies like solar and wind power. Environmental scientists stress that proactive habitat conservation and aggressive political policies are mandatory to mitigate the long-term impacts of global warming before the damage to our planet becomes entirely irreversible.
