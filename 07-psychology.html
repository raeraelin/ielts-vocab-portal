📖 Reading Passage: Theories of Perception

Human behavior is shaped by complex cognitive processes that dictate how we perceive our surrounding environment. When we experience an external stimulus, our minds immediately search for a meaningful association with previous memories. This mental processing happens so rapidly that we are rarely conscious of the underlying calculations taking place within our brain.
